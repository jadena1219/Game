# Knight's Last Stand — Story & World Bible

*Our north star. Everything we build should serve this. Names in [brackets] are
placeholders — rename freely.*

---

## Logline

A knight descends through the dying underworld to reclaim the sovereign he is
sworn to — only to learn that his king already traded him away: his life, given
willingly, is the price the kingdom paid to survive. The Demon Lord keeps raising
him from death because the bargain is not sealed until the knight **chooses** to
stay. Every descent is the Lord — and the truth — wearing him down toward that
choice.

---

## The Spine

- **The fusion (why he descends):** His sovereign was dragged into the dark, and
  the realm is collapsing without its heart. Saving the one he loves **is** saving
  the kingdom. The personal goal and the epic goal are the same act.
- **The loop (why he keeps rising — our fourth wall):** The Demon Lord revives
  him after every death. Why? Because the king's bargain only seals when the
  knight **dies willingly — kneels, accepts, stays.** So the Lord lets him rise,
  again and again, and grinds him down: through despair, through bargains, through
  the truth. The roguelite death-loop *is* the antagonist's plan. The Lord is
  meta-aware and comments on your runs, your deaths, your stubbornness.
- **The twist (The Impossible Trade):** The realm was already doomed by the Demon
  Lord's rising. The king made the only deal he could — **sacrifice his greatest
  champion to buy everyone else's survival.** He wept sealing it. He is not a
  villain; he made a monstrous, loving, unforgivable choice. The knight reaches
  the bottom to "save" a king who sold him — and who *needs* him to die.
- **The question the player carries:** *Do you accept the sacrifice?*

## Principal Cast

- **The Knight (you) — [Sir ___].** The king's ward, an orphan raised into the
  realm's greatest champion — "a son in all but blood." Loyal past reason. His
  oath is his identity; the game is what that oath costs.
- **The King — [King Aldreth].** The sovereign. Beloved, dying-realm desperate,
  guilt-drowned. Not cruel — trapped. The emotional core of the betrayal.
- **The Demon Lord — [the Pactlord].** Not a beast — a *dealmaker*. Patient,
  intimate, fourth-wall-aware. He revives you and whispers you toward kneeling.
  The most interesting villains believe they're being merciful.
- **The Dark Knight — [Sir Garrick, the First Sacrifice].** The king's *previous*
  champion, who descended to "save" him before you, and was the trade before you.
  Now a husk in the Lord's service. **You are not the first. You will not be the
  last.** A mid-descent boss whose reveal foreshadows your fate.
- **The Sorcerer (camp NPC, exists).** Reframe as an exiled court mage / remnant
  of the order who follows you down — your lore-giver and the one human voice that
  reacts to your progress across runs.

## The Descent — Five Realms

The Demon Lord's domain festers *beneath* the kingdom and corrupts upward. Each
realm shows the kingdom's fall and leaves a trace of the truth. (Currently ~20
floors; we'll pace a boss per realm and remap the existing Dark Knight / Demon
Lord fights into this frame.)

| # | Realm | Feel | Boss | What it reveals | Blade |
|---|-------|------|------|-----------------|-------|
| 1 | **The Fallen Bailey** | the kingdom's own broken undercroft; stone, crypt | [the Gate Warden] | the realm freshly fallen — your dead brothers-in-arms, familiar banners | — |
| 2 | **The Emberforge Deep** | molten mines & forges where the bargain was struck | [a fire boss] | the deal was *forged* here; the king's seal in the slag | 🔥 Emberbrand |
| 3 | **The Weeping Reaches** | frozen halls of grief & memory | **The Dark Knight** | the First Sacrifice — "he too came to save your king" | ❄️ Frostfang |
| 4 | **The Howling Vault** | a storm-wracked chasm, lightning & rain | [a storm boss] | the king's voice on the wind, no longer his own | ⚡ Stormedge |
| 5 | **The Throne of Ash** | the Demon Lord's seat, where the king "is" | **The Demon Lord** | the truth, the king, and your choice | — |

**Blade unlocks live here (answers the unlock question):** each Living Blade is a
relic of the order bound to a realm. **Beat that realm's boss once → that blade
is unlocked in the Shrine, forever.** Deterministic, thematic, and it makes the
locked swords in the shrine a *promise* ("won from the Reaches"), not a slot
machine. You always start with one blade so the opening choice is real.

## The Meta-Narrative — how it unfolds across runs (Hades-style)

The story is told *through* the loop, not in spite of it.

1. **Early runs:** You descend to save your king. The Sorcerer gives you the
   surface frame (realm falling, king taken). The Lord's whispers are cryptic.
2. **Deeper bosses:** Lore lands as you progress — your fallen brothers, then the
   First Sacrifice (Dark Knight), then the king's corrupted voice.
3. **The revival dawns on you:** At first you don't know why you keep rising. Over
   runs the Lord reveals it: *"Why do I let you stand again? Because it is not
   done until you kneel. Your king sold you to me — and you must give what was
   sold. Willingly."* The horror compounds with every death.
4. **The bottom (Throne of Ash):** The king. The truth. The choice.

The Sorcerer and the camp react to milestones (first time you reach each realm,
first boss kills, deaths) so the world feels like it *remembers* you.

---

## The Truth & The Three Endings (the final choice = replay grind)

At the Throne, you learn everything, and choose:

1. **Kneel (the Sacrifice).** Die willingly. The bargain seals; the kingdom is
   saved; you are unmade — or become the Lord's new husk. Tragic, bittersweet,
   "true to the oath."
2. **Refuse (the Defiance).** Reject the trade and fight. Win, and you break the
   deal — but the realm's salvation is forfeit unless you find another way. The
   cycle cracks; the Lord loses his hold. (Leads to the Abyss — see below.)
3. **Usurp (the Third Path).** Slay the Demon Lord and *take his power* to save the
   kingdom yourself — becoming the new dark sovereign. The Dark Knight foreshadowed
   this: *what the champion becomes.* The darkest ending, and the seed of NG+.

Multiple endings = the engine of long-term grind: players descend again for the
other endings and the "true" one.

## Systems in Service of Story

- **Souls + the Sanctum (both stubbed — light them up):** Souls are the essence
  of the fallen — your brothers, the kingdom's dead. Spend them in the Sanctum (a
  surviving remnant of the order) for power and unlocks. The Lord notes you're
  *"spending the dead."* Permanent unlocks (relics, boons, starting state) persist
  between runs and **each unlock reveals a fragment of lore.** This is grind that
  *tells the story.*
- **Cursed relics = the Lord's bargains, literal.** Power for a price, nudging you
  toward "kneel." We already built them.
- **The whispers = the Lord's voice (and, deeper, the king's).** Escalating,
  intimate. We already built the system.
- **The Living Blade + the Shrine = relics of the order.** Locked blades unlock by
  conquering the realm they belong to.
- **The Abyss (endless mode + leaderboard, late):** Canon — if you *refuse to
  choose*, you descend forever. Post-story endless run for score. Your competitive
  grind, narratively earned.

## Tone & Themes

Grim, mythic, intimate. Dark fantasy with a tragic heart. **Loyalty as both
virtue and trap. Love that damns. The cost of being someone's last hope.** Hope
threaded thin through despair — and the question of whether breaking a cycle is
heroism or selfishness.

## Build Sequencing (how we actually ship this)

Don't build the whole epic at once. Prove the engine, then expand.

- **Phase 1 — The Story Engine (vertical slice):** the intro frame (king, oath,
  goal), the Sorcerer giving story, the Demon Lord's escalating whispers, the
  **revival reveal**, **Realm 1 fully realized** with lore + boss, and the
  **Sanctum hub** working (Souls + a few unlocks). If this lands, the game has a
  soul. *(Releasable as a demo / early access.)*
- **Phase 2 — The Descent:** build Realms 2–5, the mid-bosses (greenlit!), and
  blade-unlock-by-realm.
- **Phase 3 — The Truth:** the Throne, the king, the three endings.
- **Phase 4 — The Abyss:** endless mode + leaderboard.

## Open Questions (for Jade)

1. **King or Queen?** "King + ward-son" gives Greek-tragedy weight. A queen, or a
   sibling/love instead of a parent, makes it more intimate. Pick the relationship.
2. **Names.** Knight, King, Demon Lord, the order, the kingdom — lock the ones
   that sing.
3. **How aware is the Knight?** Does he suspect the betrayal early (dramatic
   irony) or is he gut-punched with the player (discovery)? I lean discovery.
4. **Ending canon:** is there a "true" ending, or are all three equally valid?
